Welcome

Welcome to Oasis of Hope Worship Centre

Oasis of Hope Worship Centre is a Christ-centered ministry committed to raising Kings and Priests who walk in dominion, purpose, and truth. Through the Word, worship, and fellowship, we are building a generation of Kingdom ambassadors.

Learn More About Us
Point Man

Our Lead Pastor

Pastor Kayode Oladimeji is the senior pastor of Oasis of Hope Worship Centre, a dynamic ministry based upon the word of God. He has a mandate to raise a Kingdom of Kings and Priests. He believes in bringing people from nowhere to where God wants them to be.

He has served in various capacities of leadership with a proven testimony of stewardship and integrity. A major speaker in conferences and a prolific writer.
